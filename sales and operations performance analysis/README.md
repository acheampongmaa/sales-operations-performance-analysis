# **Sales & Operations Performance Analysis**

### *Excel • Google Sheets • Power BI*

---

## 📌 **Overview**

This project analyzes sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and operational efficiency using the Superstore Sales dataset. It demonstrates end‑to‑end data analytics skills using the **Google Data Analytics Process** (Ask → Prepare → Process → Analyze → Share → Act), combined with **Excel/Google Sheets for data cleaning** and **Power BI for data modeling and visualization**.

The goal is to provide insights that support strategic decision‑making and showcase practical Business Intelligence (BI) skills suitable for professional and freelance work.

---

## 🎯 **Business Task (ASK Phase)**

To understand sales trends, profitability drivers, and operational performance indicators in order to identify growth opportunities, reduce losses, and optimize business operations.

### **Key Questions**

1. How are sales and profits trending over time?
2. Which regions, customer segments, and product categories drive profitability?
3. Where is the business losing money (discounts, returns, shipping costs, product sub‑categories)?
4. Are there operational inefficiencies (long ship times, delayed orders, costly fulfillment modes)?
5. What strategic actions can improve performance in the next quarter?

### **Stakeholders**

* VP of Operations
* Sales Director
* Finance Manager
* Supply Chain Manager
* BI/Data Analyst Team

* Customer Details
* Region, Segment, City, State
* Product Category & Sub‑Category
* Sales, Quantity, Discount, Profit
* Ship Mode

Dataset placed in:


## 🧹 **Prepare & Process (Planned)**

Steps include:

* Import dataset into Excel and Google Sheets
* Remove duplicates
* Standardize data types (dates, numeric fields)
* Handle missing values
* Create derived fields (shipping time, profit margin, discount rate)
* Export cleaned version into `data/cleaned/`
